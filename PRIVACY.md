# Privacy Policy — job-search-pipeline

This is a personal, single-user tool. It runs only on the owner's own
computer and is used only with the owner's own Google account.

- **What it accesses:** the owner's Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Gmail —
  only to read/write the owner's own job-search files and to read job-related
  emails.
- **Where data goes:** job-posting text and short email summaries are sent to
  the Anthropic API for classification. Nothing is sold, shared with third
  parties for advertising, or stored outside the owner's Google account and
  local computer.
- **Other users:** the app has no other users and does not collect data from
  anyone else.
- **Revoking access:** https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
